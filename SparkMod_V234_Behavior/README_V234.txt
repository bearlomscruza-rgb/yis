SparkMod V234
SPARK CHESTPLATE
- Spark Mode is now unlocked by AWAKENING the Spark Chestplate (5 Spark Tokens at the Spark Shrine
  during a Spark Moon). An awakened chestplate turns a hit that would kill you into 10 seconds of
  immortality (Speed IV, Strength II, lightning on everyone within 24 blocks). An unawakened
  chestplate only gives its hearts. After Spark Mode you are drained until you recharge at the
  Spark Shrine (3 tokens), as before.
SPARK SWORD - LIGHTNING DASH now works three ways:
  1. Use the sword (right-click, or hold the use/interact button on touch and controller).
  2. A fallback for game versions where the sword's use action doesn't reach the script.
  3. DOUBLE-TAP SNEAK while holding the Spark Sword (works on every device).
  Dash: 7 blocks (9 awakened), 6 damage (9 awakened), 10 s cooldown (6 s awakened).
  (Fix: the sword's use hook was written in a newer item format than this pack uses.)
