SparkMod V233: structures blend into the terrain
Right after a randomly placed structure appears:
- Foundation: any gaps under the building are filled with dirt/grass, so it never floats over a dip.
- Blended slope: the ground in a 7-block ring around it is raised or cut into a smooth slope that
  meets the natural terrain (never steeper than ordinary Minecraft hills, a few gentle bumps).
- Trees the building cut through lose their floating leaves/logs near its walls.
- The reshaped ground gets fresh grass and the odd wildflower.
- Sites must now be flatter (at most 4 blocks of height difference across the footprint), so
  structures sit naturally and need little earthwork. Water is never filled in.
- The Underground Fight Pit's opening is blended into the surrounding ground the same way.
