SparkMod V224: classic Titan is back (smoother), Spark God statue
- Phases 1-3 use the original Bolt Titan model again (horns, robe, skull belt, chains, iron
  plates, rocks, chest core), repainted smoothly: every pixel is painted from its real spot on the
  body, so nothing is stretched and moss/cracks/fragments flow across the whole model.
  Phase 2 cracks still start at the chest; phase 3 is yellow with black fragments and no rocks.
- God mode swaps to a separate statue model like the reference image: tapered robe-like base,
  bronze armour outlined in glowing light, big round glowing chest, helmet with a crest, a large
  halo with ribbon arcs, glowing arm bands, a raised curved sword and a long spear, hovering.
- Models and skins are generated by tools/build_titan.py (classic shape: tools/titan_classic.geo.json).
