SparkMod V243
BOLT TITAN
- In Spark God mode the Titan now casts the Spark Bow's STORM JUDGMENT: a ritual circle of runes and
  lightning around it, then the giant beam at its target - 10 hearts through Protection IV, the giant
  lightning strike, freeze 5 s + Weakness III 10 min, and the crater (never inside protected areas,
  so not inside the Colosseum while the Titan is alive).
STRUCTURES BLEND INTO THE TERRAIN
- The Spark Colosseum and Spark Shrine now blend in too, and structures already built in your world
  are fixed once when you go near them: every gap under them is filled down to the real ground and
  the land around is shaped into a natural slope. The slope gets wider the bigger the height
  difference (a structure perched on a hill gets a whole hillside), never steeper than normal hills.
- New Colosseums sit at the middle height of the ground across the whole arena, not just its centre.
SPARK BOW
- The Spark Bow is now its own item with its own design: a pale cream-gold bow with glowing gold tips,
  grip and glint (enchanted shimmer). It shoots like a normal bow.
- Existing Spark Bows are converted automatically, keeping awakening, spent/cooldown state,
  enchantments, wear and name. Forging at the Spark Shrine makes the new bow.
