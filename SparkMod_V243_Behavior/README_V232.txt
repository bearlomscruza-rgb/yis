SparkMod V232: random structure placement
- The Spark Shrine (Temple of Sparks, X 1500 Z 1500) and the Spark Colosseum (X 1750 Z 1500) stay put.
- Every other structure now appears at a RANDOM spot while players explore:
  10 Tower & Graveyards, 4 Spark Castles, 1 Underground Fight Pit.
- Every spot is on the surface, on a plains biome or near (never on top of) a village, on reasonably
  flat dry land, fully inside the 5,000 x 5,000 Spark map (X and Z between -2500 and 2500), at least
  320 blocks from every other Spark structure and well clear of the Shrine and Colosseum.
- Nearby players get a chat message with the coordinates when one appears. Operators can list every
  structure with /scriptevent sparkmod:structures.
- Older worlds: structures already built stay where they are and count toward the totals; only the
  missing ones are placed randomly. New worlds no longer build a castle at world spawn (the 4 castles
  are all random now); a spawn castle that already exists keeps working as the spawn point.
- Structure protection and the "FOUND!" coordinate messages follow the new random positions.
