SparkMod V231: 25-second downslam cooldown
- After a landed downslam, both normal maces and Spark Maces must wait 25 seconds (was 20) before
  another downslam. Normal swings are unaffected.
- Trying to downslam during the cooldown shows how many seconds are left; a chime and
  "Downslam ready!" tell you when it's back.
- Removed a duplicated cooldown handler left over from an earlier version.
