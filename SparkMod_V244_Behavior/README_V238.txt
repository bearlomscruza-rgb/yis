SparkMod V238: SPARK BOW
GETTING IT (Spark Shrine)
- Forge Spark Bow: bring a normal bow + 8 Spark Tokens.
- Awaken Held Spark Bow: 5 Spark Tokens during a Spark Moon (counts as your awakening for that moon).
STORM JUDGMENT (awakened bow only)
- Sneak while you start drawing the bow. The ritual begins: the sky darkens, a thunderstorm rolls in,
  lightning strikes in a circle around you and glowing runes cover the ground.
  Price: 5 hearts are taken and can't be healed back for 30 seconds.
- Release to fire: a colossal beam (about 3x a beacon beam) to the player/mob you're aiming at, with a
  pillar of light crashing down on them. It deals 10 hearts straight through Protection IV and blasts
  a crater down to bedrock (never inside protected Spark structures).
- If the target survives: it can't move for 5 seconds and has Weakness III for 10 minutes.
- Afterwards the bow is SPENT: reactivate it at the Spark Shrine for 10 Spark Tokens, and the ability
  has a 10-minute cooldown. A normal draw (not sneaking) just shoots a normal arrow.
