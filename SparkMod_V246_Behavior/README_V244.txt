SparkMod V244: Spark Colosseum & Spark Shrine always on land
- The Spark Colosseum (classic spot X 1750 Z 1500) and Spark Shrine (classic spot X 1500 Z 1500) keep
  their classic spots when those are on land.
- If ocean or river is there, the structure moves to the nearest PLAINS instead (stepping into the
  plains, away from the shore). It always stays fully inside the 5,000 x 5,000 Spark map (X and Z
  between -2500 and 2500) and well away from the other one. If there are no plains in the map at all,
  the nearest dry land is used.
- Structures that are already built never move.
- The chat message when you join always shows the REAL location (and says when it was moved).
  Protection, the summoning chest, the "FOUND!" message, terrain blending and random-structure
  spacing all follow the real location.
