SparkMod V225: new Spark God model
God mode now uses a sand-gold carved-block golem: every block engraved with spiral carvings,
big shoulder blocks, white cloth sleeves and a fringed skirt with gold and blue trim, floating
carved fists set with light-blue gems, a gem ornament on the belt that pulses with the heartbeat,
a grey stone crest on the head, glowing eyes, and a glowing winged halo floating above.
It hovers gently with its fists bobbing. Phases 1-3 are unchanged from V224.
