SparkMod V246: Spark Bow upgrades
- Massive ROUND hole: the crater is a clean round pit (~50 blocks across, down to bedrock level);
  it now also clears 40 blocks above the impact so hills around it don't leave a jagged edge.
- The pillar of light from the sky stays for 20 seconds, then fades away.
- SCORCHED LAND, 250-block radius around the impact: grass becomes Scorched Grass, leaves burn away,
  tree trunks become Scorched Logs (new block, drops charcoal), grass/flowers/bushes burn into dead
  bushes. It spreads outward chunk by chunk with smoke and embers. Land that isn't loaded yet is
  scorched later when someone goes there. Protected Spark structures are never touched.
- The Spark Bow uses the normal bow's drawing animation in your hand, with three pull-back frames in
  its own pale-gold design.
- The beam now TRAVELS to the target at twice the speed of a full-power arrow, wrapped in spinning
  rings of light, and only hits when it arrives.
- While you charge the bow, three spinning rings of light in front of it throw sparks.
- Firing starts a thunderstorm.
These also apply when the Spark God casts Storm Judgment.
