SparkMod V249 - Indestructible Spark armour, Spark Bow rebalance, visible sky beam
================================================================================

SPARK ARMOUR (Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots)
- A rolled piece can never be destroyed. If it burns in lava or fire, gets blown up, despawns, or falls into
  the void, it comes straight back to the player who owns it. If that player is offline, it drops back at the
  last safe spot where it was lying.
- Only one of each piece exists in the world. Once rolled, nobody else can roll it, ever. That stays true when
  the owner leaves the game, and the piece is never freed up again.
- Creative or command copies are still deleted instantly.
- Landing on a Spark item that someone already owns (any armour piece, the Spark Mace or the Spark Sword) now
  gives you a RANDOM Spark armour piece that nobody owns yet. Only when all four armour pieces are owned do
  you get the normal loot instead. That also resets your pity counter.

SPARK BOW
- Players hit by the beam lose exactly 8 hearts, no matter their armour, Protection, Resistance or other effects.
- Every other mob takes 500 hearts of damage, which kills Wardens and Withers in one shot.
- The travelling beam is bigger, and the rings around it are twice as big.
- The beam over the crater is bigger: a 30-block core inside a 42-block glow. It now starts at the impact
  instead of at bedrock, so the game actually draws it (before, it was too far away to be seen). It is now
  translucent gold with scrolling streaks.
- Larger gold rings (18-24 blocks across) float up the beam, and a glowing particle pillar backs it up.
- Still stays 20 seconds, then fades.
