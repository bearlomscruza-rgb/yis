SparkMod V223: Spark God statue look
In god mode the Titan now looks like a glowing golden statue:
- dark bronze armour with every plate edge and seam outlined in glowing yellow light,
- glowing bands around the arms, a V of light running down the torso,
- a big round glowing chest core that pulses with the heartbeat,
- a halo of light behind its head and a crest on top,
- a curved golden sword in its right hand and a long golden spear in its left.
The tendrils hide while it is a god. (This replaces the V222 black-with-veins look and ribcage.)
