SparkMod V219: SPARK GOD MODE
When the Possessed Bolt Titan (final phase) drops to 30% health it cannot be pushed past
30% - instead (once per fight):
 1. It SHINES so brightly the screen flashes gold and it turns to broad daylight.
    A wave burns every grass block within 32 blocks into black Scorched Grass and kills
    grass, ferns and flowers (permanent). Scorched Grass drops dirt.
 2. It drives both arms into the ground and looks up at the sky (golden skin, new pose).
 3. A beam fires into the sky and the SUN TURNS BLACK (eclipse with golden corona, dark fog).
 4. SPARK GOD MODE: immortal for 20 seconds, attacks faster, golden halo.
    The boss bar shows "SPARK GOD [IMMORTAL]" with a countdown.
 5. Halfway through, the sun cracks and EXPLODES: everyone gets Poison II for 30 seconds and
    it becomes night. Night lasts for 5 straight Minecraft days (100 minutes of play time;
    sleeping can't skip it; it survives saving and reloading).
 6. God mode ends and the Titan can be finished off.
The Titan is immortal from the moment the ritual starts until god mode ends.
New sounds: shine, eclipse, godmode, sun_explode. New block: sparkmod:scorched_grass.
Note: the scorching really changes blocks in your world - test on a copy first.
