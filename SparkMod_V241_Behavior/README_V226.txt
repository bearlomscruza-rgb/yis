SparkMod V226: magma golem phases
- Phase 1 and phase 3 are now a hunched magma golem: dark volcanic rock split by glowing lava
  cracks, huge shoulders with flames burning on top, a small glowing head sunk between them,
  carved copper bands on the forearms and giant fists faced with copper bricks.
  Phase 1 is mostly cool crust (chunks of crust crumble off at 60% health); phase 3 burns hotter,
  with more and more molten lava showing as its health drops, and black crust flaking off.
- Phase 2 stays the classic Bolt Titan with glowing cracks spreading from the chest.
- God mode stays the carved sand-gold golem with the winged halo.
- Removed the leftover parts of the old Warden-style model (tendrils, ribcage, soul heart, the
  Warden heartbeat sound - god mode now uses its own synthesized heartbeat).
