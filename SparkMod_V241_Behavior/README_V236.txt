SparkMod V236
- When you join, chat shows the Spark Colosseum (X 1750, Z 1500) and Spark Shrine (X 1500, Z 1500)
  coordinates (with their height once they've been built).
- New Spark roll odds (same with or without a Spark Moon):
    God Helmet 20%, God Chestplate 20%, God Leggings 20%, God Boots 20%, Spark Mace 15%,
    Spark Sword 1.5%, God Villager 0.5%, God Apple 1%, Ore 1%, Cloud Loot 1%.
  Spark items that already exist in the world can't be rolled. Landing on one gives a still-unclaimed
  God Armour piece instead, or normal loot (apple/ore/cloud) once everything is claimed. In a new
  world nothing is claimed, so every Spark item can be rolled. A destroyed/voided God Armour piece
  becomes rollable again (V229). The 15-token pity still guarantees an unclaimed item.
- Bolt Titan health cut by another 20%: first form 445 (enraged 666), possessed form 889 (enraged 1334).
