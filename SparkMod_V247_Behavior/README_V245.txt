SparkMod V245: the Spark Shrine beams into the Spark Moon
Whenever you awaken a Spark item (any armour piece, the Spark Sword, Spark Mace or Spark Bow):
- The Spark Shrine's altar charges up, then fires a massive golden beam into the sky at the moon
  (3 seconds at full power, then it fades), with lightning crackling up it and 6 lightning strikes
  around the shrine, thunder and screen shake.
- The Spark Moon BLAZES: a huge bright white-gold moon with a golden halo and pulsing rings for about
  10 seconds, seen by everyone in the dimension. At night it is drawn on the real moon; during a daytime
  Spark Moon it appears high overhead.
- A thunderstorm rolls in, and everyone nearby sees "THE SPARK MOON BLAZES".
