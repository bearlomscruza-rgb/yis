SparkMod V247 - Beacon-style Sky Beam
=====================================

NEW: the beam over the Spark Bow / Spark God impact site
- The beam over the crater is now a real beacon-style beam rather than particles.
- It is yellow/gold with streaks that scroll upward like a beacon, plus a soft outer glow.
- It is about 50x the width of a regular beacon beam: a ~20-block-wide core inside a
  ~26-block glow, rising from bedrock to the top of the sky.
- It grows into place as the shot lands. It stays at full size for 20 seconds, shrinks away
  over 2 seconds, and is then removed.
- Gold rings and sparks still swirl around the base while it stands.
- The Spark God's judgment beam uses the same effect.
- The beam can't be hurt or targeted. Spark Sword chain lightning, the lightning dash,
  Spark Mode and the Bolt Titan all ignore it.
- Any beam left behind after a world reload is cleaned up automatically.

Everything from V246 is unchanged.
