SparkMod V216: Bolt Titan boss overhaul (vanilla boss feel)
- Summoning: invulnerable while it digs out (like the Wither charging up), world-wide spawn roar,
  "BOLT TITAN - Storm Guardian of the Colosseum" title card, and a knockback blast when it breaks free.
- Thunderstorm rolls in while the Titan is alive; clears when it is finally defeated.
- Boss bar darkens the sky, shows from 100 blocks, and renames with each phase:
  BOLT TITAN -> [CRACKED] -> SPARK DEMON -> POSSESSED BOLT TITAN -> [OVERLOAD].
- On-screen phase announcements at each phase change.
- Regenerates 1% HP per second if no survival player is within 40 blocks for 15 seconds.
- Immune to fall, suffocation and drowning damage.
- Final death plays a 5-second Ender Dragon-style show: light beams, explosions and an XP fountain,
  +500 XP to every player within 96 blocks, then the loot drops.
