SparkMod V221: horns, cracks, eyes, crumbling rocks
- Horns are smaller and symmetric (the long right-horn extension is gone).
- Cracks start at the chest: a glowing Spark Core appears on his chest when he enters phase 2.
  Stage 1: cracks only around the chest. Stage 2: they spread down the arms, face and robe.
  Stage 3: he is cracking apart everywhere, legs and horns included. Each stage triggers a burst
  of light, sparks and lightning from the core and an on-screen warning.
- Eyes: bigger, angry slanted eyes with a white-hot slit, mirrored per side, plus a soft yellow
  glow in front of them in the world.
- Rocks on his body crumble into dust and sparks as he gets low: one group at 60% HP,
  another at 35%, the last at 20%.
- Possessed form starts with its rocks and only its chest cracked, then breaks down the same way.
