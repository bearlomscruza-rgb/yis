SparkMod V227: lightning golem
Phases 1 and 3 are now a lightning-themed golem: dark storm-rock split by glowing yellow-white
electric cracks, zig-zag lightning bolts crackling up from both shoulders beside copper lightning
rods, electricity glowing between the copper bricks of its fists, and an electric glare in its
eyes. In game, lightning arcs between the two rods over its head and sparks leap off its shoulders.
Phase 3 carries more and more charge as its health drops (low HP: almost pure energy).
Phase 2 (classic Titan) and god mode (carved golem) are unchanged.
