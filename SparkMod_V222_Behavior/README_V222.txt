SparkMod V222: Warden-style Bolt Titan
- New model: a Warden-style body at 1.3x Warden size (~4.25 blocks tall) - broad head with
  swaying tendrils, long arms, short legs - still wearing the Titan's robe, gold, skull belt
  and rocks. Model + all skins are generated by tools/build_titan.py.
- Phase 1: stone slabs with moss, brown robe with gold trim, skull belt (cleaner, smoother).
- Phase 2: amber, glowing cracks spread outward from the chest's Spark Core in 3 stages.
- Phase 3 (Possessed): no rocks; bright yellow, and as it weakens black fragments spread over
  its body and flake off as falling black shards.
- Spark God: completely black with yellow lightning veins, a Warden-style ribcage over a
  pulsing glowing soul heart, heartbeat sounds, faster tendril twitching, lightning crawling
  over the body.
- If no player dies during god mode, the Spark God can't hold its power and explodes when god
  mode ends (this counts as defeating it). If it kills someone, it weakens and the fight goes on.
