SparkMod V230
BOLT TITAN
- New ability, ELECTRIC SPIKE GRAB: a warning circle appears under you, spikes of lightning burst
  out of the ground and lift you into the Titan's crackling grip, it holds you in the air for about
  1.5 seconds, then hurls you away (damage on lift and on throw; you can't move while held).
- The Titan keeps its phase-1 look (the lightning golem) through phases 1, 2 and 3. When it becomes
  the Spark God it switches to the god form - and keeps the god form for the rest of the fight.
- 1.5x bigger (hitbox enlarged to match).
- Better close-range melee: reach scales with its size and is measured along the ground, it can
  hit you even when you're right against or under it, and every swing sweeps everyone hugging it.
- When the sun goes black, the whole sky goes dark (midnight + dark fog) and stays dark until the
  Titan dies. (The 5-day night after it destroys the sun still applies afterwards.)
SPARK SWORD
- New sounds: electric slash on every hit, chain-lightning crack, Lightning Dash thunder.
- Every hit cuts a lightning slash through the target; awakened swords sometimes arc to a 2nd enemy.
- 10-hit critical chain: stun lasts 3.5 s inside an electric cage, and chain lightning jumps to up to
  3 more enemies (4 damage, 6 awakened).
- NEW: LIGHTNING DASH - use (right-click / hold use) the sword to dash 7 blocks (9 awakened) as a bolt
  of lightning, hitting every enemy on the path for 6 (9 awakened) and briefly stunning them.
  Cooldown 10 s (6 s awakened). Teammates are never hit.
